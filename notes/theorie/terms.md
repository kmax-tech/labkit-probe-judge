# Terms of this lab

*The lab's glossary — the one home of a term that more than one area, script
or note uses (LAB-084 Double-coinage check: field first, then this table,
then the existing stock; what is found is inherited, never coined twice).
Method terms (criterion, admissibility condition, registered branch) live in
the lab standard's vocabulary table, tool terms (answering run, stamp) in the
labkit README, a term of one protocol in its field "Terms of this protocol".*

One row per term. The everyday word is what a reader without the lab's
vocabulary would say; the lab term is the word the notes use; the code word
is the identifier, flag or field; the field term is what the literature calls
it, with the source when there is one.
